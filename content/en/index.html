THE MEASUREMENT-TO-DECISION CHAIN

A parameter needs context.

WAT is evidence about defined monitor structures under specified conditions. It does not, by itself, identify a process cause or establish product disposition.

Scope: monitor design and coverage · parameter and extraction definitions · validity and uncertainty · hierarchy and coordinates · bounded engineering hypotheses · WAT, in-line and electrical die sort evidence limits.
